ReadVolley puts the official volleyball and beach volleyball rules in your pocket, together with everything a referee looks up around them.

Indoor and beach
- The rules, chapter by chapter, with the casebook rulings and the referee guidelines and instructions next to each rule
- Rules under experimentation in the current Swiss season are marked
- Court and play diagrams, definitions and the referee hand signals
- Game protocols, plus the beach protest, ball mark, medical assistance and forfeit protocols
- Search across rules, cases, guidelines, definitions and protocols

Swiss Volley
- News from the Swiss referee commission (SSK)
- The 2026/27 rule changes, in English
- Documents and links for national league referees

Works offline
The rules data is downloaded once and kept on the phone, then refreshed in the background at most once a week. Images and documents are loaded from readvolley.openvolley.app when you first open them and kept for offline use.

ReadVolley is an independent reference made by referees. It is not an official publication of FIVB or Swiss Volley; for official decisions, the federation documents are binding.
